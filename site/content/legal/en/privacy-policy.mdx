## Who we are

The website kreatekno.com is run by <LegalName />, located at <OfficeAddress />. On this page, "Kreatekno", "we" and "us" mean that company.

For data you send to us directly, for example when you ask for a consultation, Kreatekno is the **personal data controller**. This means we decide why and how that data is processed.

This policy follows Indonesia's Law Number 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (the PDP Law) and its implementing regulations.

## Data we receive

**While you read this website**, you don't need to sign up or fill in a form. Two kinds of technical data are still recorded:

- **Visit statistics**: the pages opened, where the visit came from (for example a search engine), the type of device and browser, the country, and which buttons were clicked, for example the Free consultation button. These statistics are collected without cookies and without names. The visit marker changes every day, so your visits cannot be followed from one day to the next.
- **Server logs**: the IP address and browser details the hosting provider uses to deliver pages and to protect the website from attacks.

**When you contact us** by chat from the Free consultation or Meet in person buttons, by Instagram, or by email, we receive:

- the name and phone number, account name or email address you use;
- the messages and attachments you send, for example a description of your business, photos or documents;
- the time and place of a meeting if you ask to meet in person.

Please don't send data that the consultation doesn't need, such as ID card numbers, health data or personal financial data.

**When you become a client**, we also receive the data needed for the service agreement and invoicing, for example the business name, the name of the person in charge, the address and the business tax number.

## What the data is used for

- Answering your questions and preparing the consultation.
- Scheduling meetings, by call or face to face.
- Preparing quotes, agreements, invoices and work reports if you become a client.
- Sending follow-up messages about the consultation you asked for. You can ask us to stop at any time.
- Understanding which pages are useful so we can improve this website. For this we only look at combined numbers, not at data per person.
- Keeping the website secure and meeting legal obligations, such as tax obligations.

We don't sell or rent out your personal data. We also don't use your data for ads that follow you to other websites.

## Legal basis

The PDP Law requires a lawful basis for all processing (Article 20). The bases we use are:

- **Your request before and during an agreement.** You contact us for a consultation, and we process the data needed to answer you. If you become a client, data is processed to carry out the service agreement.
- **Your consent**, for example when you send documents or photos for us to study. You can withdraw this consent at any time.
- **Legal obligations**, for example keeping invoices for tax purposes.
- **Legitimate interests**, namely visit statistics and website security. Data for these purposes is not used to identify you.

## Other parties that process data

We use services from other companies. They process data only to provide their service to us:

- **Meta Platforms**, the provider of WhatsApp and Instagram, for conversations from the Free consultation and Meet in person buttons and for Instagram messages. WhatsApp messages are also subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy, and Instagram messages to Instagram's privacy policy.
- **Vercel**, the host of this website, to deliver pages, keep server logs and count visits without cookies.
- **Our email provider**, for emails you send to us.
- **Work tool providers**, for example document storage, spreadsheets and accounting software, to keep records of consultations and client work.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it, for example on an official request from law enforcement.

## Data stored outside Indonesia

Some of the providers above run servers outside Indonesia, including in the United States. In line with Article 56 of the PDP Law, we only use providers that offer equivalent protection or protection that is binding by agreement, and we inform you of these transfers through this policy.

## How long data is kept

- **Consultation chats that don't lead to a project**: at most 2 years after the last message, then we delete them.
- **Client data**: for as long as the project runs. After it ends, financial documents such as agreements and invoices are kept for as long as company document and tax rules require, usually 10 years. Other data is deleted.
- **Visit statistics**: kept as combined numbers that cannot identify you.
- **Server logs**: kept briefly by the hosting provider under its own policy.

If you ask for earlier deletion, we follow your request, except for data the law requires us to keep.

## How we protect data

- Only the team members who handle your consultation or project can open your data.
- Our work accounts are protected with strong passwords and two-step verification.
- This website can only be opened over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
- We never ask for your account passwords by chat or email. If a project needs access to your business systems, that access is agreed together and removed when it is no longer needed.

If a breach affects your personal data, we inform you in writing within 3 x 24 hours of learning about it, in line with Article 46 of the PDP Law. The notice explains which data was exposed, when and how it happened, and what we are doing about it.

## Your rights over your personal data

Under Articles 5 to 13 of the PDP Law, you have the right to:

- be told who we are and on what basis and for what purpose we process your data;
- access and get a copy of your personal data, including in a machine-readable format;
- complete, update or correct data that is wrong;
- ask us to stop processing, delete or destroy your data;
- withdraw consent you have given;
- ask us to pause or limit processing;
- object to decisions made entirely by an automated system;
- sue for and receive compensation if your data is processed unlawfully.

Send your request to <PrivacyEmail /> with the subject "Personal data request", or by chat. Before acting on a request, we make sure it really comes from you, for example by matching the number or email you used before.

We give access, correct data, stop processing after consent is withdrawn, and pause processing within 3 x 24 hours of receiving the request. For deletion, we let you know once the data has been deleted.

## Data of our clients' customers

Kreatekno builds AI assistants and automations for client businesses, for example an assistant that answers customer chats or records orders. Inside those systems, customer data is processed **on the client's instructions**. For that data, the client business is the controller and Kreatekno is the **data processor**.

- We process our clients' customer data only to run the system agreed in the service agreement.
- Third-party services the system uses, for example AI model providers, servers and chat apps, are chosen together with the client and listed in the agreement.
- When a project ends, we return or delete the customer data as the agreement says.

If you are a customer of a business that uses a system built by Kreatekno, send requests about your data to that business. We help the business fulfil your request.

## Children's data

Our website and services are meant for adult business owners and workers. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us. Under Article 25 of the PDP Law, we only process children's data with the consent of a parent or guardian. If we learn that we received a child's data without that consent, we delete it.
